# AutoLedger

Trip, charging, fuel, maintenance and cost bookkeeping for your cars, in the Home Assistant sidebar.

- Log refuels, odometer readings, maintenance and other costs (insurance, road tax, parking…).
- See km, cost per km, L/100 km and monthly costs per car or for all cars together.
- Export everything as CSV.

Automatic trips and charge sessions from your car and charger entities arrive with the AutoLedger Collector integration (coming soon).
