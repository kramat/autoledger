# AutoLedger

Trip, charging, fuel, maintenance and cost bookkeeping for your cars, in the Home Assistant sidebar.

- Every drive, charge and refuel of a connected car recorded automatically with the **AutoLedger Collector** integration, priced with your live electricity price.
- Cars without Home Assistant data logged by hand: refuels, odometer readings, maintenance and other costs.
- km, cost per km, L/100 km and kWh/100 km per car or for all cars, year reports, cars side by side and trends.
- Maintenance schedules with reminders in Home Assistant, receipt photos, depreciation, Excel export, backups and CSV import.
- In English and Dutch, on desktop and phone.
